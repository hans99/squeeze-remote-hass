	EN	Home Assistant connect URL

	EN	Enable Assistant plugin

	EN	Home Assistant Access Token

PLUGIN_ASSISTANT_PASS_DESC
	EN	A long-lived access token from Home Assistant (Profile → Long-Lived Access Tokens).

	EN	Connection status

PLUGIN_ASSISTANT_STATUS_CONNECTING
	EN	Assistant is starting or not connected to Home Assistant.

PLUGIN_ASSISTANT_STATUS_CONNECTED
	EN	Connected and authenticated to Home Assistant.

PLUGIN_ASSISTANT_STATUS_DISCONNECTED
	EN	Not connected to Home Assistant.

PLUGIN_ASSISTANT_STATUS_EMPTY
	EN	No Home Assistant areas/entities available yet.

PLUGIN_ASSISTANT_ERR_CONNECT_REQUIRED
	EN	Home Assistant connect URL is required.

PLUGIN_ASSISTANT_ERR_CONNECT_INVALID
	EN	Connect URL must start with http:// or https://
